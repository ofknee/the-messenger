> "this is a very tuff game" -socrates

## NOTES

### blurb
welcome to "Pay to Play" aka "Build a Bear Game"!<br><br>
what truly makes a game fun? does ease or difficulty perpetuate more dopamine?<br>would you make your own playthrough harder if certain factors compelled you to do so? 
and more importantly, how hard are you willing to work for your own enjoyment?<br><br>
lock out of doomscrolling and get the minimal or maximum dopamine you can get out of this lil meta-platformer-game :D

### controls
WASD or arrow keys for movement<br>
gold coins +1<br>
eyeball coins +5<br>
buy elements of a game through the shop<br>— WARNING: any unselected options in the menu will DISAPPEAR the moment you move to level 2, so buy wisely

### tech stack
the wonderful godot<br>
+ a bit of aseprite sprinkled in

### other
- landing detection, checkpoints, limiting animation --> :(<br>
